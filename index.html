About us

Different ways to experience Bali. One Soulvana.

Soulvana is a Bali-based team crafting small-group adventures and mindful retreats for travellers who want more than a checklist. Whether you are chasing hidden waterfalls or slowing down in a jungle sanctuary, every journey is designed around Bali’s nature, culture and people.

We work with local guides, families and communities, so what you experience is genuine and what you spend stays close to home.

  • Mindful retreats and wild adventures in one place
  • Small groups led by local guides
  • Journeys rooted in Balinese culture and community

Before You Begin Your

Every journey comes with questions. Find the answers you need to feel prepared, travel with confidence, and make the most of your Soulvana experience.

Finding Your Journey
How do I choose the right Soulvana experience?
Start by considering how much time you have and the kind of experience you are looking for. Our retreats focus on reflection, wellbeing, and meaningful cultural encounters, while our adventure programs offer opportunities to explore Bali's landscapes, villages, and local traditions. Each experience has its own itinerary and inclusions.
Can I join Soulvana on my own?
Yes, solo travellers are welcome to enquire about our experiences. Whether you are travelling alone, with a partner, or with friends, we can help you understand the arrangements for your chosen program, including accommodation and group format.
Reserving Your Place
What is included in the price?
Inclusions vary by experience. Depending on your selected program, the package may include accommodation, meals, ground transportation, entrance fees, guided activities, and cultural experiences. Please check the individual program page for the exact inclusions before booking.
How do I reserve my place?
Contact the Soulvana team with your preferred experience and travel dates. We will confirm availability, provide the relevant package details, and explain the payment and booking process before your reservation is finalised.
Can I request a private or customised experience?
If you prefer a more personal journey, contact us with your group size, preferred dates, and interests. We can discuss the available options and whether adjustments to the itinerary are possible.
Travel Gently, Feel Cared For
Do I need to be physically fit to participate?
The level of physical activity depends on your chosen experience. Some programs involve walking, outdoor exploration, or time in natural settings. Review the itinerary and let our team know about any accessibility needs or concerns before booking.
What happens if the weather affects an activity?
Outdoor activities depend on local weather and safety conditions. If an activity cannot proceed safely, the team will assess the available alternatives or adjustments to the schedule. Any changes will depend on the activity and conditions at the time.
Preparing for Bali
What should I bring with me?
Your packing list depends on your itinerary. Comfortable clothing, suitable footwear, sun protection, personal toiletries, and any necessary medication are useful starting points. We will advise you of any additional items required for your selected activities before departure.
Do I need travel insurance?
We recommend arranging suitable travel insurance before your trip. Check that your policy covers the activities you plan to undertake, along with relevant medical expenses, cancellations, and other travel risks.
Can Soulvana accommodate dietary requirements?
Please inform us of any dietary requirements or food allergies when enquiring or booking. We will discuss what can be accommodated for your selected program and confirm arrangements in advance.
